BEHIND THE INSIGHTS

Connected to your existing work.

The credit assessment uses your saved Random Forest classifier and target encoder. Crop yield preserves the formula from your Streamlit app; it is an estimate, not a trained yield model.

Income assumes TZS 300/kg. Repayment capacity uses 35% of estimated gross income, and expected loss assumes 45% loss given default. Loan recommendations retain the original assessment rules and do not account for interest, expenses, or a repayment schedule.

Pending subsidies are encoded as received, matching the original app. Other notebook models have no saved artifacts connected here.

Inputs are processed by the local Python server and are not stored by this app.
